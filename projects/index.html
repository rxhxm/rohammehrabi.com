A Closer Look at Neural Codec Resynthesis
NeurIPS 2024 paper from MIT CSAIL on resynthesizing speech from coarse neural audio codec tokens. I designed and ran the Mechanical Turk human evaluation, which surfaced the paper's central finding — that objective audio metrics (SI-SNR, ViSQOL, WER) diverge sharply from what humans actually hear as natural speech.
